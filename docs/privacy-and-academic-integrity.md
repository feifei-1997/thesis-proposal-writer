# Privacy and Academic Integrity

## Data Sent to CQVIP

When retrieval is enabled, the Skill sends generated literature search queries to the configured CQVIP endpoint. Queries may include the thesis topic and research subdirection. Do not include confidential experimental data, personal identifiers, unpublished results, or restricted institutional information in those fields.

The Skill does not upload the complete conversation history to CQVIP.

## Literature Claims

- Treat returned records as metadata or abstracts unless authorized full text was actually obtained.
- Do not invent or complete missing bibliographic fields.
- Verify important claims against the original publication before submission.
- Keep in-text citations and the reference list consistent.
- Respect CQVIP account permissions, licensing terms, and usage limits.

## Generated Writing

The output is a draft, not an academic authority. Users remain responsible for factual verification, research ethics, originality, disclosure of AI assistance where required, and compliance with institutional policies.

## Logs and Artifacts

Do not persist API keys, authorization headers, private papers, or personal research data in runner input, generated examples, CI logs, issues, release archives, or screenshots.
